# Support

## Bug reports and feature requests

Use the GitHub issue tracker. Include the Mustatil version, operating system, Python version, installation method, relevant log output, and reproducible steps.

## Research interpretation

Mustatil generates and manages candidate detections. The project cannot confirm chronology, cultural attribution, legal site status, or archaeological significance. Those questions require qualified domain review and, where applicable, permission-based field investigation.

## Sensitive location data

Do not post confidential or legally protected site coordinates in public issues. Use a synthetic or redacted example whenever possible.

## Response expectations

Mustatil is maintained independently and without guaranteed response times. Security-sensitive reports should follow `SECURITY.md` rather than a public issue.
