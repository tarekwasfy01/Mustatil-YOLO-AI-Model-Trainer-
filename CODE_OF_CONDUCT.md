# Code of Conduct

Participants must communicate respectfully, focus criticism on ideas and code, avoid harassment or discriminatory behaviour, and protect sensitive research or location information shared for debugging. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation when necessary.

For a widely used external standard, contributors are also encouraged to follow the principles of the Contributor Covenant: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
